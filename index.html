Region 3 Autumn Regional Meeting 2018

This year a Regional Meeting is organised by BEST Zagreb. Students from Graz, Ljubljana, Maribor, Mostar, Novi Sad, Timisoara, Veszprem and Zagreb are meeting in Croatia to prepare themselves for the upcoming General Meeting. The meeting is taking place in Omanovac from the 18th to the 21st of October where students will be able to discuss all the important subjects in peace and enjoy nature in their free time. The Omanovac Mountain Lodge is located on one of the most beautiful slopes of Psunj Mountain and it is just the right place for this meeting to be held because of its spaciousness and beautiful, peaceful environment. Regional Meeting is an internal BEST event whose primary purpose is to prepare delegates for Presidents’ Meeting France 2018 by familiarizing them with important topics and current situation in other LGBs. Participants are also involved in interesting training sessions for developing soft skills and hard skills. During a Regional Meeting it is also very important for people to get to know each other better, to build stronger relationships between LGBs and to have a lot of fun!
